Peer-reviewed research and academic sources used to provide information about psychopathy, sociopathy, and Antisocial Personality Disorder.

How These Sources Were Used

These sources were used to provide research-based information about psychopathy, Antisocial Personality Disorder, assessment methods, personality traits, gender differences, and the relationship between psychopathy and criminal behavior. Academic research was used to distinguish psychological concepts from common misconceptions presented in popular media.

Additional Research

Research on psychopathy and ASPD continues to develop. New studies continue to examine how these conditions are defined, assessed, and understood in different populations.
